Margins

Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Margins is a document editor for macOS. It saves documents on your Mac. You can choose to sync them with Google Drive or use an AI provider to help edit them.

What Google Drive sync accesses

When you connect Drive, Google asks you to approve access to the specific files you use with Margins. This permission is called drive.file. Margins creates a folder called Margins in the Google account you select.

Sync copies complete Margins documents between your Mac and that folder. A document can include text, images, instructions, conversations, annotations, and previous versions. Margins reads file names, identifiers, revisions, and changes to match copies across devices and detect conflicts.

Where your data goes

Your Mac communicates directly with Google for sign-in and sync. Margins stores the tokens that authorize sync in your macOS Keychain. It keeps documents and a sync index on your Mac. The developer operates no server that receives your documents or tokens during sync.

If you ask an AI provider to work on a document, Margins sends your instruction and relevant context to the provider you select. This can include text from a synced document. The provider’s data practices apply to that request. Syncing a file by itself does not send it to an AI provider.

Disconnecting and deleting data

  1. To stop sync, open Settings → Sync in Margins and disconnect Google Drive on each Mac.
  2. To revoke the app’s permission, remove Margins from your Google account connections.
  3. To remove stored documents, delete the copies you want to remove from Drive and from your Macs. Disconnecting leaves those copies in place. Google Drive and macOS provide separate Trash controls.
